# Global Discount & Approval Policy - Version 8.0

## Purpose & Scope
This commercial governance policy applies to all global sales transactions processed under Framework 8.

## Delegation of Authority (DoA) Matrix
* **Account Executive:** Maximum discretionary discount of up to 7% off list price.
* **Regional Sales Director:** Authority to approve discounts between 8% and 21%. Requires signed justification memo.
* **Vice President of Global Sales:** Authority to approve discounts up to 27%.
* **Executive Committee (CRO/CEO):** Any discount exceeding 30% or containing non-standard indemnification requires board-level review.

## Compliance
Failure to log discount rationales in the CPQ audit ledger within 24 hours results in commission forfeiture for the deal cycle.
