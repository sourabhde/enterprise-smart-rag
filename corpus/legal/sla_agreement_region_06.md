# Master Service Agreement - Region Code 06

## Section 6: Uptime Guarantees and Service Level Agreements (SLA)
6.1 **System Availability:** Vendor commits to a monthly uptime availability of 99.15% for all production workloads deployed under Region 06.

6.2 **Credit Remedies:** In the event of availability breaches, client receives service credits:
* Availability between 99.0% and 99.8%: 15% credit of monthly MRR.
* Availability between 95.0% and 98.9%: 30% credit of monthly MRR.
* Availability below 95.0%: 50% credit of monthly MRR plus termination rights.

6.3 **Liability Cap:** Aggregate liability for Region 06 contracts is strictly capped at 100% of fees paid during the preceding twelve (12) months.
