# Enterprise Product Suite - Module 15 Specification

## Executive Summary
Module 15 delivers advanced data orchestration, automated quote-to-cash workflows, and high-performance ingestion pipelines tailored for enterprise tier-1 organizations.

## Pricing Structure & Tiered Licensing
* **Base Platform Subscription:** $57,500 / year.
* **Included User Seats:** 25 named users. Additional seats billed at $1750 per user/year.
* **Volume API Tiering:**
  * Tier 1 (0 - 15M calls/mo): Included in base fee.
  * Tier 2 (15M - 50M calls/mo): $0.00012 per request.
  * Tier 3 (50M+ calls/mo): Requires custom enterprise rider approval.

## Contractual Terms & Renewals
* Automatic annual renewal with a standard 4% inflation adjustment cap.
* Multi-year commitments of 36 months qualify for a 12% total contract value (TCV) reduction.
