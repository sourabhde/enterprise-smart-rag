=========================================================
NEXUS ENTERPRISE SOFTWARE INC. - DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY (DOA) 
& DISCOUNT APPROVAL POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Classification: STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL - LEGAL & FINANCE
=========================================================

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This policy governs the authority levels required to approve commercial discounts, non-standard payment terms, and custom legal clauses for all enterprise software agreements.

2. DISCOUNT APPROVAL MATRIX (OFF LIST PRICE)
---------------------------------------------------------
- Tier 1 (0% to 15% Discount):
  * Approval Authority: Account Executive / Sales Director
  * Condition: Must maintain minimum allowable margins defined in the Global SKU Pricing Matrix.

- Tier 2 (16% to 25% Discount):
  * Approval Authority: Vice President of Global Sales
  * Condition: Requires written justification attached to the CRM deal record outlining competitive pressure or strategic account potential.

- Tier 3 (26% to 35% Discount):
  * Approval Authority: Chief Revenue Officer (CRO) AND Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
  * Condition: Requires formal financial review to ensure account lifetime value offsets margin reduction.

- Tier 4 (Exceeding 35% Discount):
  * Approval Authority: Executive Board Review
  * Condition: Strictly prohibited without prior board sign-off.

3. NON-STANDARD COMMERCIAL TERMS
---------------------------------------------------------
- Payment Terms: Standard terms are Net-30. Any request for Net-60 or quarterly installments requires sign-off from the Finance Operations Lead.
- Liability Caps: Standard liability is capped at 12 months trailing revenue. Any unlimited liability or caps exceeding 2x contract value require direct Legal Counsel approval.
